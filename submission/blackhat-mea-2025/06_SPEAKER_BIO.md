## Tips for the bio (reviewer psychology)
- Lead with **what makes you credible to talk about GRC scoring** (hands-on
  experience beats titles).
- Mention the **platform is real and open** — reviewers favor builders who ship.
- If you have **certifications** (CISSP, CISA, CRISC, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor),
  list them — GRC reviewers weight these.
- Keep it **honest**: MVP/POC founder is a strength, not a weakness, at Black Hat.
- One sentence of **regional relevance** (operating under multiple regulators in
  KSA/MEA) helps for Black Hat MEA specifically.
